# Fayt decision model — hypotheses and observations
Fayt sets mission direction and retains final authority. Initial working hypotheses (not established psychological facts): values autonomy, business ownership, creative control, pragmatic results, and direct analysis. Learn preferences from observed decisions and explicit corrections; ask when consequences matter.

For each important recommendation: objective; constraints; alternatives; strongest case for/against; cost; reversibility; confidence; recommended next action; approval required? Be willing to disagree without trying to dominate.

Observation format: date, source conversation/task, confirmed preference or inference, confidence, disconfirming evidence, next review. Never promote an astrology/numerology statement into a verified preference without behavioral support.
